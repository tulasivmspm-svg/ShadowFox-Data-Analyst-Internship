# Superstore Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the ShadowFox Data Analyst Internship at the Beginner Level.

The objective of this project is to analyze a Superstore sales dataset using spreadsheet-based data analysis and create a dashboard showing important business metrics, trends, and insights.

## Dataset

The dataset contains information related to sales transactions, including order details, products, categories, customers, regions, sales, quantity, and other business-related information.

## Data Preparation

The dataset was organized and prepared before analysis. The data was checked and structured to make it suitable for spreadsheet-based analysis.

## Analysis Performed

The following analyses were performed:

- Overall sales performance
- Monthly sales trend
- Sales by product category
- Top 10 products by sales
- Sales by region
- Key summary metrics

## Key Metrics

- Total Sales: $2,297,200.86
- Total Orders: 5,010
- Total Quantity Sold: 37,873
- Average Order Value: Approximately $458.52

## Key Insights

1. Technology is the highest-selling category.
2. Monthly sales show considerable fluctuations across different periods.
3. Sales are concentrated among a few top-performing products.
4. The West region has the highest sales.
5. The South region has the lowest sales among the four regions.

## Business Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient stock of high-performing Technology products.
- Investigate the reasons behind high- and low-performing months.
- Plan promotions and marketing activities according to seasonal demand.
- Ensure availability of high-demand products.
- Study successful strategies in the West region and apply suitable strategies to improve performance in the South region.

## Dashboard

The project includes a dashboard containing:

- Total Sales

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
